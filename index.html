How to play

Drag with your mouse to look around.
Click the glowing rings to travel or interact.

Listen

The world responds to your actions. Books, mechanisms, and unseen passages all have a voice. Pay attention to what you hear.

Audio

Adjust music and sound levels with the ♫ button. Each can be muted independently.

Hint

The Keepers' library has more than one way out. Some are obvious. Others reward observation.
